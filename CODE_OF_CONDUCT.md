# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want this project to be a welcoming, harassment-free space for everyone who
uses or contributes to it, regardless of experience level, background or identity.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that helps the community:

- Being respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests and discussions.
- Reporting broken or stale data clearly, with enough detail to reproduce.
- Assuming good faith and focusing on the problem, not the person.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory comments.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Using the project's data or discussions to promote spam, abuse, or illegal activity.

## Responsible Use

This repository publishes a public snapshot of free proxies for legitimate
development, testing and research. Do not use the project or its community spaces
to coordinate spam, attacks, credential abuse, or bypassing access controls. See
[SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md) and the responsible-use note in the README.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues and pull
requests that violate this Code of Conduct. To report a concern, open a
confidential report through GitHub's private reporting or the contact listed in
[SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md).
