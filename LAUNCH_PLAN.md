## Free vs Pro Boundary

Free repository:

- Public snapshot.
- CDN links.
- Basic quality score.
- No signup.
- No SLA.

Pro API:

- Fresh checks within 60 seconds.
- Filtering by protocol, country, latency and quality.
- Rotating endpoint.
- Higher concurrency.
- Usage dashboard.
- Alerts and support.

The free layer must remain useful. The paid layer sells reliability, freshness,
filtering and support.

## Risk Controls

- Keep responsible-use language in README and website.
- Remove sources on request.
- Avoid claims about bypassing specific platforms or access controls.
- Do not market public proxies as exclusive, residential or production-grade unless they actually are.
- Do not require stars, follows or purchases to access the free repository.
